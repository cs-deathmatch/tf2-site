Thanks for choosing to play on my server! If you enjoy, don't forget to favorite it and tell a friend!

Here on Pink Pwny Club (pronounced Pony, like the song), we've got a vanilla casual experience, with just a handful of plugins to help the rounds flow better. Tired of the map? Rock the vote! Want to flame the enemy? No need to type faster than a league player, all talk is enabled.

Currently we only have vanilla maps in rotation, but I'm looking into adding a handful of custom maps. To see what we have enabled, or to suggest a map to add, check the Maps tab.

There's also a handful of chat commands available to fine-tune your experience, such as !maps, !votecrits, and !votescramble. Check the Info tab for more information!

Detailed rules are available on the page above. But in short; don't be an asshole, and have fun.

If you have any questions, want notified for events, or just wanna hang out, hop in the Discord!
