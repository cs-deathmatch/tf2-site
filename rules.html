2: Sprays are a privilege, not a right. You must be a regular member on the server to have spray perms, and they can be taken away if you abuse it (nsfw, gore, etc)
3: Soundboards are fine, but please keep the audio to a respectable level. and if somebody asks you to stop, stop. Additionally, only one person may be playing music at a time.
4: People are actually trying to play the game here. This isn't a 24/7 2fort chill hangout y'know? Have fun, play the way you want. But if you're actively not playing, don't be shocked when you're kicked.
5. Do not abuse our moderation systems. Repeated spam of the report forms, or usage of !calladmin when nothing is wrong, is not allowed and will be punished.
6: Air Bud clause. Just because something isn't explicitly disallowed, doesn't mean you won't be punished for it. Don't be the reason I have to make a new rule...

Overall, I'm just trying to create a fun and safe space for everyone to have a good time. If you see somebody breaking the rules, type !calladmin in chat to let us know! If they're no longer doing something obviously against the rules, and you need to file a report, you can do so with thisform.
